# Silhouette

Silhouette is a free, open-source desktop app for Windows and macOS. It reads your work calendars through their private ICS links and copies them into a Google Calendar you own. Each meeting becomes a plain busy block, such as `Work - Acme`, with no meeting details.

You can share that calendar with family or a partner. They see when you're busy, but not what you're doing.

- Runs entirely on your computer. There is no Silhouette server, account or telemetry.
- Writes only a title and a start and end time for each event.
- Only ever changes events it created itself.
